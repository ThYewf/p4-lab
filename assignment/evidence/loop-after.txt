C4 — Result and Interpretation

A single tunnelled packet with destination ID 3 and initial hop limit 8 was injected into the same forwarding loop used in C1.

The packet-processing counters increased from 661 to 666 on s1 (+5) and from 659 to 663 on s2 (+4). After a further three seconds, both counters remained unchanged.

The nine packet-processing events correspond to eight successful forwarding operations and one final drop at s1 when the hop limit reached zero. The s1 log confirms execution of `MyIngress.drop` and `mark_to_drop(standard_metadata)`.

Neither destination host received the looped packet.

Conclusion: The modified P4 program successfully terminates the forwarding loop, whereas the original implementation continued forwarding packets during the observation period.
